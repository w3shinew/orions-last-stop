PERSONNEL & DECK DIRECTORY · PUBLIC ACCESS

Know your way
around the station.

From the first docking ring to the quiet observation deck, every corner of Orion’s Last Stop has a purpose — and usually a story attached to it.

01 / THE CREW

The people keeping the lights on.

K-7 “KEPLER”Android · Head of Hospitality. Handles reservations, table service and guest requests. Polite to a fault; secretly keeps a list of the station’s worst jokes.
ZEE VORTANZorblaxian · Head Mixologist. Inventor of the Nebula Bloom and several drinks that are still being studied by the science deck.
NOVA REYESHuman · Flight Deck. Former cargo pilot who knows the trade lanes, the shortcuts and which warning lights can be ignored (none of them).
02 / DECK DIRECTORY

Explore the station.

DECK 01 · DOCKING RING

Arrival control, customs, public airlocks and the east-airlock exchange desk.

DECK 02 · MAIN LOUNGE

The central cantina, guest booths, live transmissions and the main bar.

DECK 03 · RECIPE VAULT

Ingredient archive, recipe terminal and the kitchen synthesizer.

DECK 04 · REPAIR BAY

Droid maintenance, nav-unit diagnostics and cargo patch facilities.

DECK 05 · QUIET OBSERVATORY

Panoramic windows, low-noise seating and long-range navigation views.

DECK 06 · CREW QUARTERS

Private crew spaces. Visitor access is restricted unless invited by a crew member.

03 / LOCAL LEGENDS

Stories told after midnight.

Regulars swear the station was built around the engine core of an ancient survey ship. Others claim the neon sign is older than the trade corridor itself. Zee says both stories are true, depending on who is paying.

The house jukebox is rumored to contain a recording from every ship that has ever docked here. K-7 denies this, but has never explained why it occasionally plays songs that nobody recognizes.

04 / NEED A HAND?

Ask the crew.

For menu recommendations, speak to Zee. For docking, reservations or lost property, find K-7. For route advice, ask Nova — preferably before she goes off shift.

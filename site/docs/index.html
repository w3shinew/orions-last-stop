01 / ARRIVAL

Find your way to Dock 07.

The cantina orbits a quiet trade corridor beyond the mapped shipping lanes. Look for the three amber navigation lights and follow the blue approach beacon to the public landing ring.

  1. Approach the beacon.Reduce to sublight speed at the outer marker and request a standard docking slot.
  2. Clear the airlock.Visitors pass through a routine atmosphere and cargo scan. Keep personal navigation devices with you.
  3. Follow the music.The main lounge is two decks down. You cannot miss the neon sign, the music, or Zee arguing with the espresso synthesizer.
02 / CURRENCY

How Star Credits work.

Star Credits (SC) are the house currency. Menu prices are displayed in SC, and the exchange desk accepts most recognized trade tokens from nearby systems.

01 · PAYMENTS

04 / HOUSE ETIQUETTE

Keep the galaxy friendly.

Respect other guests, do not interfere with station systems, and follow crew directions during docking or emergency procedures. The cantina is neutral ground: settle arguments with words, not blasters.
